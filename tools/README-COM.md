## menu

A single entry point to the ecosystem.

I got tired of signing in to every app separately. If they're all part of one
ecosystem, moving between them should be seamless. I was also fed up with typing
addresses into the browser — it's easier to keep every app in one place and
get to menu from any of them.

## What's inside

- **SSO across apps** — sign in once and your session follows you, no repeated logins
- **Every service at hand** — each app is one click away, and any of them leads back to menu
- **Availability check** — menu keeps an eye on the apps and won't send you to one that's down
- **Info center** — everything about the ecosystem in one place
